The Story

An illusion built on fire, patience and a little smoke.

Mirage began as a single wood-fired hearth and an obsession with the moment a dish disappears. Today, twelve courses unfold in near-darkness, each one lit only by the flame that made it.

We source within 90 kilometres, ferment in-house, and let the seasons write the menu. Nothing is repeated twice.
