The Almas Story

A diamond carved from silence, salt and stone.

In Arabic, almas means diamond — and like a diamond, this place was shaped slowly, under pressure, by forces far older than us.

What began as a single fishing lodge on a forgotten stretch of the Red Sea is now a 62-villa sanctuary where architecture bows to the landscape. Local limestone, reclaimed timber, hand-thrown ceramics — nothing here shouts. Everything here endures.

No lobby music. No queues. No schedules unless you ask for one. Just the sound of the tide, the smell of cardamom coffee, and a staff who remember your name before you do.
